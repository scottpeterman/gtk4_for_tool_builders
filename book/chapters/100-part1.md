# Part I: Foundations {.part}

Part I builds an empty but correctly made window: an application that starts
and stops properly, widgets whose ownership you can explain, a layout shaped
like tmssh's, and settings that survive a restart. There is very little to
look at yet. What you get instead is the ground every later chapter stands
on: the application lifecycle, GObject's reference counting and signals, the
container model, and a core library that keeps GTK out of the logic.
